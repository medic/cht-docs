## Highlights

### Archiving

Long running or very active deployments can be overwhelmed with huge documents counts. These documents in CouchDB are no longer relevant in day to day operations: CHW's don't need them and analytics in CHT Sync and Couch2PG have already aggregated them.  However, every CHT upgrade will process these older docs, adding heavier CPU use and longer upgrade times. Outside of upgrades, CouchDB indexes will track all the older docs, ever increasing disk space and CHWs logging in for the first time will need to download years worth of data.

CHT 5.3 introduces archiving to address these issues. It enables CHT administers to provide a list of documents to a new CHT API which instructs Sentinel to move the documents out of the main CouchDB database to a cold storage CouchDB database. This safely keeps them out of indexes, decreasing upgrade time, decreasing first sync time and overall decreasing disk space because CouchDB indexes will be smaller. Existing data in CHT Sync and Couch2PG remains untouched ensuring business analytics numbers don't change.

Read more in [the documentation](https://docs.communityhealthtoolkit.org/technical-overview/data/performance/archiving/) or the [original GitHub issue](https://github.com/medic/cht-core/issues/6615).

### Reducing hosting total cost of ownership

Along with high document counts highlighted above, disk space continues to be one of the key pain points CHT administrators face when running the CHT over longer periods of time.  5.3.0 adds a number of critical CouchDB index improvements which result in both more faster re-indexing during upgrades and, critically, less disk space used after upgrading to 5.3.

For those that love technical details, see [the original GitHub tickets](https://github.com/medic/cht-core/issues/10651).  Otherwise, there is no documentation for this feature - it works as is when you upgrade!

### Photos on Sub-contacts

CHWs often have many visits to fit into an already busy day.  Any form entry that can be sped up is always welcome.  From the community came need was specified to be able to attach a photo to a sub-contact which is created while registering a new household.  In-lining this process greatly reduces the amount of time it takes to fill out a form and removes the tedium of going into each contact after it's created to attache a photo.

### Contact card photos

Accompanying the ability to attache photos to sub-contacts is the ability to configure the CHT to show a small thumbnail icon for each contact.  Humanizing those who CHWS care for is important - these patients are not just a name in a database, but a real person who needs help.  Further, photos can disambiguate people with similar or identical names. This is another feature requested by and created by the community!

### External datasets in forms

Deployments that use large lists in their worflows have long struggled with CHTs inability to re-use these lists between forms.  For example, a list with 100s of choices that is used in 10 forms would mean CHWs need to download list 10 times, greatly increasing sync times when ever this list is updated.  For admnistrators, if one change is needed, they need to be sure to propigate out to the other 9 copies of the list.

CHT 5.3 massively simplifies this issue by enabling the re-use of lists in forms.  These shared datasets are declared once in app settings and then can be used as many times as needed.  We're happy the community requested [this feature](https://forum.communityhealthtoolkit.org/t/select-choice-question-type-with-a-lot-of-choices/3261?u=mrjones) and it is finally arriving to serve their needs.
